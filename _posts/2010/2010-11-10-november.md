Autumn has the power to bring people back together, to bring nature closer to human hearts. It feels like crying inside and laughing outside — and at the same time, enjoy everything in between.

**![Help Net](/images/november-help.jpg)**

**![Western Union](/images/november-western.jpg)**

November definitely has something special: that electrical feeling that makes everything stand up by itself, even if it's just a sign posted on a wall.

**![Piraeus Bank Bucharest](/images/november-office.jpg)**

**![Domino Steps](/images/november-glass.jpg)**

Even buildings don't remain indifferent when this month makes its appearance. It's like watching that drama film that you saw tens of times in the past, but now you feel like you're watching it for the first time — and you're noticing those peculiar details, here and there, in almost every scene. November is marvelous.
