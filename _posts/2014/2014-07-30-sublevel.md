---

[Subreply](http://subreply.com/) is to Twitter what Pinboard is to Delicious, Reddit to Digg, Linux to Windows, Android to iOS, and so on.

Here are the project requirements for [Subreply](http://subreply.com/):

* **No JavaScript.** Subreply works with JavaScript disabled.
* **Dumb phones supported.** Subreply works with Opera Mini.
* **Responsive design.** Subreply works on any device with a modern browser, from smartphones to TVs.
* **Elegant design.** Subreply needs to feel as good as — or better than — a native app.
* **Powerful web app.** Subreply uses design hacks to improve performance.
* **Different than Twitter.** Subreply is not a clone of Twitter the way App.net was, nor is it trying to reinvent the wheel. There is no character limit; Subreply is limited by design only.
* **No user tracking.** Subreply doesn't store any metadata associated with users or replies.
* **Discoverable.** Subreply uses an explore page to keep people informed, similar to HN, DN or Reddit.
* **Clear searching.** Subreply lets you independently search for replies or for people's usernames, bios and locations.
* **Just text.** Subreply won't provide support for images, videos, gifs or mp3s — third-party services do a better job. Subreply provides support for links, mentions and hashtags.
* **Threaded conversations.** Subreply's reply system is clear, so there is no need for mentions. It lets you join any conversation.
* **Less spam.** Subreply prevents spam with two features. First, it lets the parent of a reply delete any children replies. Second, it lets people send just one reply, expecting answers back from the other participants in the conversation.
* **No API.** Subreply doesn't need an API, just better web browsers. Instead of an API, Subreply will try to have an open database.
* **Powered by mini ads.** Subreply is free, and the ads will never be intrusive — actually, they look quite good.

The idea behind Subreply is to have a public network with no hidden tricks, gimmicks or any other crap like that.

Have some fun on [subreply.com](http://subreply.com/) :)
